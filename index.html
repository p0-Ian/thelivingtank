The Living Tank Support

The Living Tank is a realistic aquarium game: you set up Carter's 40-gallon tank, grow the bacteria that make it safe, stock it, and keep it healthy for a month and beyond.

Getting started

  • Tap the title screen, then use the four buttons at the bottom: Feed, Observe, Maintain, Build.
  • The button at the top right opens Home: the store, your journal, field notes, goals and settings.
  • A new tank needs to cycle before fish move in: Maintain > Treat > Ammonia, and a used sponge from the store speeds it up. Observe > Test water tells you when ammonia and nitrite read zero.
  • Tap the clock to skip to the next morning or a week ahead. The game pauses itself when something happens.

Common questions

  • My fish died. Why? Open the journal and tap Why? on the entry: the autopsy shows the last three days of the water and what would have prevented it.
  • Does time pass when I close the app? In Gentle mode (the default) no. In Hobbyist mode (Settings) your tank lives on while you are away.
  • Can I start over? Settings > Start over drains the tank and begins again.
