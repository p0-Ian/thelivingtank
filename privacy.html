The Living Tank Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

The Living Tank is a single-player aquarium game with a simple privacy story: we do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. None.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. The game contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or accounts, and it makes no network requests to us or anyone else.

Data stored on your device

To remember your tank, the game saves its state on your device only: the water, plants and fish in your tank, your journal and field notes, your in-game wallet, a small photo of your tank on day 30, and your sound and haptics settings. This never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app erases it.

Children's privacy

The game is made for kids and grown-ups alike and collects no data from anyone, including children.
